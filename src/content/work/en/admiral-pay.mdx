## Context

Admiral Pay is a digital payment institute with over 40,000 partner retailers across Italy. The platform handles prepaid cards, phone top-ups, bill payments, PagoPA, gift cards, event tickets, and gaming account operations (deposits, withdrawals).

The existing app had accumulated multiple problems: onboarding with effective completion time of 48-96 hours before account activation, features that no longer worked as intended, layered code, low CRO, poor usability compared to competitors.

## What I did

As Lead Designer of the project:

- **Complete heuristic analysis** of the app in production based on Nielsen's 8 criteria (cognitive load, user control, error prevention, typographic hierarchy, visual consistency) + **Satispay benchmarking** on design patterns, visual hierarchy, and onboarding flow
- **Mapping of two complete user journeys** with step-by-step user feedback, emotional curve, and intervention notes
- **Product goal redefinition** across three areas: strengthen brand identity, streamline onboarding, improve overall user experience
- **Onboarding flow redesign** with fast NFC identification (like Satispay, Apple Pay, Fineco), PIN activation, reduction of required data during registration (postponing non-essential fields to after activation)
- **3 co-design sessions** with multidisciplinary client teams + weekly standups (Scrum/Lean approach)
- **Complete interface redesign and new design system** built to scale to other group products

## Result

The redesign is now in production. Onboarding time dropped from **48 hours to 5 minutes**. Account activation CRO **doubled**. The design system has been reused across other Admiral group digital products.
